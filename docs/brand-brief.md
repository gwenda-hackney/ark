## Theme

**The Archive Salon · On the Canal**

Gwenda is a fused after-hours **archive salon** on Schwartz Wharf—not a bar with food, or a restaurant with drinks, but one room where **culinary craft, mixology, art, and music** hold equal weight.

- **The archive** — rare elevated street food and craft cocktails; regional traditions most venues never serve
- **The salon** — intimate, low-lit, conversation-first; a room that holds still
- **The canal** — canalside on the water; warehouse setting, not a nautical theme pub
- **Art** — work from Wick studios on the walls; sparse, considered, never permanent
- **Music** — vinyl on the decks; lounge-first, not the dance floor

Gwenda may be a woman, a vessel, or both. We can't say.

## Public naming and labels

_How we name Gwenda in titles, meta tags, listings, press, and link previews._

**When a venue type is needed, use Ristobar.** Gwenda is more than the label—but _ristobar_ is the one category word we stand behind for discovery: Italian food and drink as equals, not a bar with a kitchen or a restaurant with a drinks list.

| Context                                | Label                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Primary name**                       | **Gwenda** — always                                                                                               |
| **Public category**                    | **Ristobar** — titles, SEO, listings, press when a short type helps                                               |
| **Location**                           | Hackney Wick · Schwartz Wharf · canalside                                                                         |
| **Page titles & SEO**                  | `Gwenda — Ristobar` + `\| Hackney Wick` (+ season/canalside as space allows)                                      |
| **Link previews & short descriptions** | Canalside ristobar + pillars: craft cocktails, hyper-regional Italian food, art, vinyl                            |
| **Story / on-site body copy**          | _archive salon_ (lowercase) when **explaining** the room—after the negation; theme language, not the public label |
| **Theme (internal)**                   | _The Archive Salon · On the Canal_ — strategy, briefs, mission                                                    |

**Do not use as a primary public label**

Archive Salon · restaurant · bar · club · lounge · cocktail bar · Italian restaurant

**Examples**

| ✓ Use                                                                                              | ✗ Avoid                                    |
